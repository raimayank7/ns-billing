How to make a GST invoice here

  1. Type your business details once. Name, address, state and GSTIN. Add a logo, signature, bank details and UPI ID under "Logo, signature and bank details". Next time, start typing your business name and pick it from the list.
  2. Choose the type. Tax invoice if you are GST-registered and charge GST. Bill of supply if you are a composition dealer or the goods are exempt. Invoice (not GST registered) if you have no GSTIN.
  3. Add the customer. For a registered buyer, type the GSTIN: the state fills in from its first two digits. Saved customers appear as you type their name.
  4. Add items. Description, HSN or SAC code, quantity, unit, rate, discount and GST rate. Items you have used before appear as suggestions, with their last rate.
  5. Check the preview, then Save. Press Print / PDF and choose "Save as PDF" to make a PDF, or Share to send the amount and payment details on WhatsApp.

What it does for you

  • Right tax, every time: CGST + SGST for a sale inside your state, IGST for a sale to another state, and UTGST for Chandigarh, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Andaman and Nicobar, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.
  • Invoice numbers that follow the rules: a new number for each bill in the series INV/26-27/001, restarting each financial year, within the 16-character limit. It warns you if a number is used twice.
  • Tax summary by HSN, amount in words in the Indian style (lakh and crore), round off, reverse charge, ship-to address and the Original / Duplicate / Triplicate label.
  • Get paid faster: add your UPI ID and the invoice shows a QR code with the exact amount filled in.
  • Hindi and Punjabi: the printed labels can be in English, हिंदी or ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
  • Works offline once the page has opened, on phone and computer. Install it from your browser menu ("Add to Home screen" or "Install app") to open it like an app.

Your data stays with you

Everything you type is kept in this browser on this device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us. Your businesses, customers and items are kept in one place on my.niveshsahayak.in, so every Nivesh Sahayak document tool can suggest them.

Keep a backup. If the browser's data is cleared, saved work in it is gone. Open My data (top of the page) and press Save a backup file now and then, or on a computer choose a folder (a OneDrive or Google Drive folder is best) so every save is backed up automatically. The page reminds you after 7 days without a backup.

Questions people ask

Is this invoice maker really free?

Yes. Making, saving, printing and sharing invoices is free, with no login and no limit on the number of bills. A small line at the bottom of the printed bill says where it was made.

Where are my invoices saved?

In your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Save a backup file from My data so your work is safe if the browser is cleared, and use the same file to move your data to another phone or computer.

Does it work out CGST, SGST and IGST?

Yes. When your state and the place of supply are the same, it splits GST into CGST and SGST (UTGST in Union Territories without a legislature). When they differ, it charges IGST. Read CGST, SGST or IGST? for the rule.

Can I make an e-invoice with IRN here?

No. An e-invoice IRN can only come from the government Invoice Registration Portal. If e-invoicing applies to your business, report the invoice there. See E-invoice and e-way bill.

How do I make a PDF?

Press Print / PDF. In the print window, choose Save as PDF as the printer. On Android, choose "Save as PDF" from the printer list; on iPhone, pinch out on the preview and share it.

Is the tax worked out correctly for my goods?

The maths follows the rate you type. The rate itself depends on your goods or service and changes when the GST Council decides. Check it with your accountant or on the official CBIC rate list.
