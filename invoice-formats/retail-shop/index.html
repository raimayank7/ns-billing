Retail shop bill format with GST

Most shop bills go to consumers who are not registered under GST (B2C). The rules are lighter for them, but a few details still matter.

What a shop bill needs

  • Your shop name, address and GSTIN, invoice number and date.
  • Item description, quantity, rate, discount and GST rate for each line.
  • Customer details only when the bill is ₹50,000 or more (name, address, delivery address, state). For smaller bills, the customer's name and phone are useful but not required.
  • HSN codes are optional on B2C bills if your turnover is up to ₹5 crore, but showing 4 digits keeps your records tidy for GSTR-1.
  • Place of supply: for over-the-counter sales, it's your own state, so CGST + SGST.

Tips for shops

  • Turn on Round off so the total is a whole rupee.
  • Add your UPI ID once: every bill gets a QR with the amount, and customers pay in seconds.
  • Small sales under ₹200 to customers who don't want a bill can go into one consolidated invoice for the day.
  • Choose Hindi or Punjabi labels if your customers prefer them.
